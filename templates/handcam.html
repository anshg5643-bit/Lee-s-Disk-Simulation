HOLO-LAB · INPUT CALIBRATION

Control the lab with your hand.

Set up your camera, learn the pinch gesture, and troubleshoot tracking before starting the experiment. Camera access is used locally by the browser-based hand-tracking model; the lab still works with buttons and a mouse.

Open holo simulation →Ask Monika
01

Before you begin

  • Use a recent version of Chrome or Edge.
  • Open the lab at localhost or over HTTPS.
  • Allow camera permission when your browser asks.
  • Close other apps that may be using the webcam.
02

Camera positioning

  • Place the camera at about eye level and keep your hand fully in frame.
  • Use even lighting; avoid a bright window behind your hand.
  • Keep the background reasonably uncluttered.
  • Move slowly and avoid placing fingers against the edge of the frame.
03

Pinch to activate

  1. Open Simulation and choose Enable hand controls.
  2. Wait for the status to say hand tracking is ready.
  3. Bring your thumb and index finger together over a glowing target.
  4. Hold the pinch briefly (about 0.65 seconds) to activate it.
  5. To remove the steam chamber, pinch it and lift your hand slowly.
04

What each target does

Heater: starts or stops heating. Capture: records a synthetic temperature reading. Remove chamber: begins the cooling phase. The visible buttons remain available as a fallback.

This is a learning simulation. Webcam gestures do not measure real temperature or control physical equipment.

QUICK DIAGNOSTICS

Camera not responding?

No permission prompt

Click the site icon beside the address bar, open site permissions, and allow Camera. Reload the simulation page.

Camera is black

Check that another app is not using the camera, then disable and re-enable hand controls.

Hand not detected

Move your hand closer, improve lighting, keep the whole hand visible, and avoid very fast gestures.

Model fails to load

Check your internet connection because the browser loads the MediaPipe model from a CDN. If unavailable, use mouse or buttons.
