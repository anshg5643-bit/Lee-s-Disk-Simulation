Apparatus guide

Meet the instruments before you use them.

Each object below has a job in the heat-transfer measurement. Review it before stepping into the simulation.

◎

Lee’s metallic disc

The known mass and specific heat capacity make it a calorimeter. Its cooling rate gives the heat loss per second.

▤

Steam chamber

A deep hollow cylinder of the same diameter. Steam maintains an almost constant high temperature at the top of the specimen.

▱

Bad conductor specimen

A circular ebonite, cardboard, glass, wood, or polymer disc. Measure its thickness and diameter accurately.

🌡

Two thermometers

One measures steam-chamber temperature T₁; the other measures the Lee’s-disc temperature T₂.

◷

Stopwatch

Used for equally spaced readings during cooling. The clock starts as soon as the chamber is removed.

◌

Boiler and heater

Generate the steam that supplies heat to the chamber. In the virtual lab, this is the heater control.

↔

Vernier calliper

Measures the specimen diameter. The area is calculated as A = πd²/4.

⌇

Screw gauge

Measures the small specimen thickness x; zero error must be accounted for in a physical experiment.

⚖

Balance

Measures the mass m of the Lee’s disc. This is essential in calculating heat loss during cooling.
